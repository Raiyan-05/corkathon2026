CrewOps: Airport Ground Crew Management System

What does it do ?

Automatically builds ground crews and matches them to arriving flights.
Every crew has 4 people, including 1 team leader.
Each crew covers all 4 skills: tug, belt loader, baggage and steps.

How the teams are made ?

The supervisor presses Generate teams & assign.
The system uses everyone working that day who hasn’t called in sick.
It builds crews so each one has a team leader and every skill covered.
Each person is given the skill they’ll handle on that crew.

How the flights are assigned ?

Arriving flights go into a queue in order of arrival.
Each flight goes to the crew that has been free the longest during its shift.
Crews get a short break between flights.
The supervisor approves each assignment, or reassigns it to another team.

What will employees see ?

Their next flight, with arrival time, departure time and stand.
Their team and the skill they’re covering.
Today’s roster and a board of all flights.

What if you are late or sick ?

Employees report “Running late” or “Call in sick” in the app.
Calling in sick automatically suggests a free colleague with the same skill.
The supervisor approves the cover with one click.
Everyone involved gets a notification.

Technologies used: 

Back end: Python, Flask, SQLite
Front end: HTML, CSS, Jinja2, JavaScript
